I am a **College Assistant Professor in Applied Mathematics** at [St John's College, University of Cambridge](https://www.joh.cam.ac.uk/research/academics/fellows/dr-adrian-ka-wai-chung).

I completed my undergraduate and MPhil studies in Physics at the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), before obtaining my PhD in Physics from King's College London. I then joined the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign as a postdoctoral research associate in Nicolás Yunes's group. In 2025, I moved to Cambridge as a Herchel Smith Research Fellow in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics and a College Research Associate at St John's College, before taking up my current appointment in 2026.

My research is in gravitational physics and is motivated by three broad questions:

1. **What is the nature of gravity and spacetime?**
2. **What is the nature of dark matter?**
3. **How fast is the Universe expanding?**

To address these questions, my research spans:

- Black holes and strong-field gravity
- Gravitational-wave tests of General Relativity
- Black-hole perturbations and quasinormal modes
- Spectral methods for gravitational physics
- Numerical relativity and compact-binary dynamics
